Why the calculation is correct

All register arithmetic is performed modulo 2N using exact JavaScript BigInt operations. Signed values are interpreted with N-bit two's complement. The combined final register A‖Q is then interpreted as a signed 2N-bit two's-complement integer.

01 → A = A + M 10 → A = A − M 00 or 11 → A unchanged Then: arithmetic right shift of A‖Q‖Q−1

Register-width rule

Auto-detect chooses the smallest width of at least 4 bits that can represent M, −M, and Q as signed two's-complement values. The extra check for −M prevents overflow in the subtraction step when M is the most-negative value for a width. A manually selected width is accepted only when M, −M, and Q fit its signed range.

Example: 14 bits represent −8192 through +8191, so M = 1234 and Q = −5678 are valid.
